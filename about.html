Built on our name.

A family-run Edmonton contractor doing insulation, drywall, taping and texture the way it should be done.

Our story

Two brothers. One standard.

Singh Brothers Drywall Contracting Ltd. started with a simple idea: do the work like it’s going in our own home. The company is run by brothers who are on the tools and on site — so the people you talk to are the people doing the job.

We focus on one part of the build and we do it fully: insulation, drywall, taping and texture. Staying focused is what lets us deliver consistent, clean finishes on every project, whether it’s a single basement or a full house.

  • Owner-operated — you deal with us directly
  • Clear written quotes before work begins
  • Clean, protected job sites every day
  • Based in Edmonton, Alberta

How we work

What you can count on.

01
