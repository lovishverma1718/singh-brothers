Insulation01

Warm in winter, quiet all year.

Edmonton winters are hard on a house. We install insulation and vapour barrier carefully so there are no gaps, compressed batts or open seams for heat to escape through — and so your walls are ready to pass inspection before the board goes up.

Drywall02

Straight, solid and square.

A good finish starts with good hanging. We plan our layouts to keep joints to a minimum, fasten to spec and cut clean around every opening — using the right board for each room, from moisture-resistant board in bathrooms to fire-rated board where it’s required.

What’s included

Walls & ceilingsBasement developmentRenovationsNew constructionMoisture-resistant boardFire-rated board

Taping03

Seams you’ll never see.

Taping is where drywall is won or lost. We tape, mud and sand in multiple coats, set crisp corner bead, and feather every joint wide so the wall reads as one flat surface — even under raking light.

What’s included

Taping & muddingCorner beadSandingSmooth finishesPatch & repairPaint-ready walls

Texture04

Even texture, clean edges.

The final layer should look consistent from every angle. We apply ceiling and wall textures evenly, match existing finishes on repairs and renovations, and keep sharp clean lines where texture meets walls and trim.

Questions

Good to know.

Can’t find your answer? Call or text 780-707-4832 and we’ll help.

Do you give free quotes?

Yes. Call or text us, or send the form on our contact page, and we’ll set up a time to look at the job and give you a clear written quote.

Can you handle the whole job, from insulation to texture?

Yes — that’s what we do best. One crew handles insulation, vapour barrier, drywall, taping and texture, so there are no gaps between trades.

Do you take on small jobs and repairs?

We do. Along with full basements and new builds, we take on smaller renovations, patches and repairs. Get in touch and tell us what you have.

Do you work with builders and general contractors?

Absolutely. We work directly with homeowners as well as builders, developers and general contractors in the Edmonton area.

We’re based in Edmonton and work across the city and surrounding communities. If you’re unsure whether we cover your area, just ask.
